COCKRELL'SAUTO CENTER
Shop board / working demo

Every car, from drop-off to pickup.

Enter a car, watch it move through the fifteen steps, and see the shop at a glance. Everything below is live, and every car and name is made up.

1. Input

When a car comes in, the front desk fills in this form. It takes about a minute. Try adding one.

2. Status

Each car is a card, and each column is a step in the repair. Drag a car forward as the work gets done. New cars start in Uncategorized.

3. Reports

The service manager's view: cars in the shop, late cars, what each car is waiting on, and who has room for more work.
