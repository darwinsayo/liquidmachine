Shop board / working demo

Every car, from drop-off to pickup.

Enter a car, watch it move through the fifteen steps, and see the shop at a glance. The form is live, so you can try it, and every car and name is made up.

1. Input

When a car comes in, the front desk fills in this form. It takes about a minute. Try adding one.

Each car is a card, and each column is a step in the repair. As the work gets done, the card moves forward to the next step.
