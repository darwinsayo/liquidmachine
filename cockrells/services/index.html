Home  /  Services What we do

Collision repair, paint, and towing under one roof.

From the tow truck to the final polish, your vehicle stays with one shop and one team.

01  /  Body repair

Restored to its pre-accident condition and specifications.

Certified technicians and modern frame equipment bring your vehicle back to how it was built. Today's vehicles take more skill and more equipment to repair than ever, so we keep our technicians trained and our shop current.

  • I-CAR trained and ASE certified technicians
  • Structural damage repaired on our frame rack
  • Sheet metal repair and replacement
  • Specialist work such as air bag replacement
02  /  Paint and refinish

A finish that blends with the color around it.

Every panel to be refinished goes through a thorough preparation process before it sees paint. The finish goes on in a downdraft, heated spray booth, then the exterior is polished to match the original factory finish and texture.

  • Two downdraft paint booths
  • Waterborne paint, matched to factory color
  • Polished to the factory finish and texture
03  /  Towing

Can't drive it? We'll bring it in.

If your vehicle can't be driven after an accident, we tow it to our repair center. 24-hour tow service is available on request, so you don't have to wait for the next business day to get started.
