Home  /  Repair process Repair order / an average collision repair

Fifteen steps, none skipped.

The more damage from the accident, the more complex the repair. This is what happens to your vehicle, in order, from estimate to the day you drive it home.

Part one

Estimate, approvals, and parts

01
AdministrativeThe estimate is written, and insurance and owner approvals are obtained.
02
PartsIn most cases, parts are ordered before the vehicle goes into production.
03
DisassemblyDamaged parts come off, and any hidden damage is listed for the customer service representative.
04
Revised estimateThe estimate is updated for newly found damage and sent to the insurance company.
05
Additional partsMany times, more parts need to be ordered once the estimate is revised.
Part two

Repair and refinish

06
Structural repairsDamage to the inner structure is repaired on our frame rack.
07
Cosmetic repairsSheet metal repair and replacement are done after the vehicle leaves the frame rack.
08
Refinish preparationEvery panel to be refinished goes through a thorough preparation process.
09
RefinishingThe finish is applied in our downdraft, heated spray booth.
10
PolishingThe exterior is polished to match the original factory finish and texture.
11
ReassemblyDoor handles, moldings, and trim go back on in the body shop.
12
Mechanical repairsSpecialist work, such as air bag replacement, is performed at this stage.
Part three

Finish and return

13
DetailingThe vehicle is cleaned thoroughly inside and out.
14
Quality controlEvery repaired area is checked closely, and most vehicles are road tested.
15
ReturnWe call you, you pick up your repaired vehicle, and any deductible or payment is collected.
Good to know

We'll walk you through it.

Our goal is to make the collision repair process a pleasant experience. Our professional staff will walk you through every step of getting your vehicle repaired.
