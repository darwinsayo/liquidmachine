Repair order  /  an average collision repair

Fifteen steps, none skipped.

The more damage from the accident, the more complex the repair. This is what happens to your vehicle, in order, from estimate to the day you drive it home.

01
Administrative The estimate is written, and insurance and owner approvals are obtained.
02
Parts In most cases, parts are ordered before the vehicle goes into production.
03
Disassembly Damaged parts come off, and any hidden damage is listed for the customer service representative.
04
Revised estimate The estimate is updated for newly found damage and sent to the insurance company.
05
Additional parts Many times, more parts need to be ordered once the estimate is revised.
06
Structural repairs Damage to the inner structure is repaired on our frame rack.
07
Cosmetic repairs Sheet metal repair and replacement are done after the vehicle leaves the frame rack.
08
Refinish preparation Every panel to be refinished goes through a thorough preparation process.
09
Refinishing The finish is applied in our downdraft, heated spray booth.
10
Polishing The exterior is polished to match the original factory finish and texture.
11
Reassembly Door handles, moldings, and trim go back on in the body shop.
12
Mechanical repairs Specialist work, such as air bag replacement, is performed at this stage.
13
Detailing The vehicle is cleaned thoroughly inside and out.
14
Quality control Every repaired area is checked closely, and most vehicles are road tested.
15
Return We call you, you pick up your repaired vehicle, and any deductible or payment is collected.
