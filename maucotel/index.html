About

An accomplished and inquisitive performer.

Jared is deeply interested in exploring and sharing music with all audiences. He maintains a performing career in both solo and chamber music, and has taught and managed his own violin studio for more than a decade.

Cincinnati Conservatory of Music, with Kurt Sassmannshaus
